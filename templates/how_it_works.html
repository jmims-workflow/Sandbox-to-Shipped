How it works

{{ APP_NAME }} is where anyone at Northstar asks for an AI tool, finds out whether we already have one, and sees the decision with a plain reason. Here's the path a request takes.

The path a request takes

  1. Request{{ icon("arrow", 18) }}
  2. Check what exists{{ icon("arrow", 18) }}
  3. Reviewer decides{{ icon("arrow", 18) }}
  4. {{ icon("flask", 16) }} Being tried{{ icon("arrow", 18) }}
  5. {{ icon("check", 16) }} Approved

From sandbox to shipped: a new tool starts as being tried by one team, and moves to approved once it proves itself.

What we promise

  1. One front door. Requests, decisions and approved tools live in one place, so nobody has to chase an answer in chat.
  2. Check what exists first. Before you submit, we show approved tools that may already do the job. Often the best answer is a tool we already have.
  3. A person decides every request. Claude sizes up requests, suggests matches and drafts reasons. It never approves or turns down anything on its own.
  4. Every "no" comes with a reason and a path. Each decision says why, and what would change the answer.
  5. We ask what you actually know. The form asks how often and who would use it, not how many hours it would save, because hours are hard to guess.
  6. Past decisions are open, requesters stay private. Decided before shows every past decision and its reason so nobody asks twice. It never shows who asked.
  7. Owners agree to be owners. Nobody is listed as a tool owner unless they agreed to it.
